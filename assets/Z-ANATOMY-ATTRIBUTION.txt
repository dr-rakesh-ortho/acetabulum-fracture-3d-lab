Modifications: selected anatomical bone meshes, omitted annotation and muscle
attachment objects, cartilage and sinus cavity objects. Omitted both hip bones,
sacrum and coccyx to avoid overlapping the separate NIH pelvis. Reoriented,
reflected and uniformly scaled for approximate display alignment; reversed
triangle winding. Vertex coordinates rounded to 0.001 mm. No decimation.
Converted to gzipped indexed triangle JSON. Full inventory and transform details
are provided in z-anatomy-manifest.json; exporter is in source/export-z-skeleton.py.
This is not a patient-specific or validated cross-dataset anatomical registration.

The Z-Anatomy skeleton is kept as a separate asset from the pre-existing NIH
fracture pelvis. NIH pelvis assets retain their own CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licence
and attribution; see NIH-ATTRIBUTION.txt. This notice does not relicense them.
